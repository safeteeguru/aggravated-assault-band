AGGRAVATED ASSAULT PERFORMS LIVE ON HARD @ LURK!

November 3, 2026 | Band News

Bay Area thrash metal band Aggravated Assault brought loud and fast to Hard @ Lurk on Monday, November 2, 2026, performing tracks from their highly anticipated upcoming EP, The Faceless, scheduled for release in January 2027!

Following the performance, the band sat down with host Kate Vail for a conversation covering the band's history, musical influences, the making of The Faceless, and what's next for Aggravated Assault.

And the band may have let something slip! Along with discussions about upcoming shows, they dropped hints about a possible Southern California tour in 2027 to promote their new album! Stay tuned as more details emerge.
